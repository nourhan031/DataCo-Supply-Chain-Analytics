# DataCo Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing supply chain 
performance, delivery efficiency, and customer behavior for 
DataCo Logistix.

## Key Insights
- 'Consumer' segment drives ~52% of total revenue
- Europe and LATAM are the top performing markets by revenue share
- Deliveries are ~57% late, signaling fulfillment inefficiency
- Repeat customer rate sits at ~59%, showing strong but improvable retention
- Revenue shows a slight declining trend across months, reflected in the -4.03% Sales Growth Rate


## Tools Used
- **Power BI** — data modeling, DAX measures, visualizations
