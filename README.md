# DataCo Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing supply chain 
performance, delivery efficiency, and customer behavior for 
DataCo Logistix.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview
- Total Revenue, Orders, Sales Growth Rate, Avg Fulfillment Time, Profit Margin, Repeat Customer Rate
- Revenue trend by month 
- Avg Fulfillment Time by Shipping Mode
- Profit by Product Category
- Revenue breakdown by Customer Segment

### 2. Deliveries
- Avg Fulfillment Time, Total Deliveries, Profit Margin, Unique Products
- Count of Orders by Month
- Deliveries by Delivery Status (Late, Advance, On Time, Canceled)
- Deliveries by Payment Type (Debit, Transfer, Payment, Cash)
- Total Orders by Order Status

### 3. Customers
- Total Customers, Repeat Customer Rate, Avg Orders Per Customer, Avg Customer Lifetime Value
- Total Orders by Customer Segment (donut chart)
- Top Customers table by Revenue, Order Count, and Segment

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Power BI Desktop** — data modeling, DAX measures, visualizations

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Consumer segment drives ~52% of total revenue
- Late deliveries are the most common delivery status (~57%), signaling fulfillment inefficiency
- Repeat customer rate sits at ~59%, showing strong but improvable retention
- Standard Class shipping has the highest average fulfillment time at ~3.5 days
